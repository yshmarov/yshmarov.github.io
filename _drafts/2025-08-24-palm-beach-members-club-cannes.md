### The swimming pool

The pool is beautiful, the views are fantastic. The pool uses filtered seawater. You don't feel chlorine in the water. Swimming there feels great.

Kids are allowed in the pool only until 15:00. So if you have kids that go to school, there is no chance to visit with them on weekdays after school.

I have a small child that does not go to school yet, so we used to visit in the early mornings.

As a basic member, you can invite one adult guest + kids to the pool. This was nice, because the two of us could take other friend with kids to enjoy the pool with us.

2024.

Renting a sunbed cost EUR75-100 for members.

Swimming pool closed at 18:00.
